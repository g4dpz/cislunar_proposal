# LinkedIn Retirement Announcement

I've retired from commercial space communications and am now focusing on amateur satellite projects.

**RADIANT Project (Radio Amateur Delay-tolerant Interplanetary Networking Testbed):** Developing Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN) for amateur radio - the same protocols NASA uses for deep space missions. Starting with terrestrial packet radio, progressing through QO-100 geostationary satellite, then LEO CubeSat missions, with a proposed cislunar space mission. DTN enables store-and-forward messaging across disrupted links with long delays.
https://radiant.amsat-uk.org

**FUNcube+:** Next-generation amateur satellite payloads building on the FUNcube heritage. Provides educational telemetry and transponder capabilities for universities and schools, with improved power efficiency and more flexible operations than previous generations.
https://funcube.org.uk

**Other projects:**
• µD3TN and picoDTN experiments for embeded systems
